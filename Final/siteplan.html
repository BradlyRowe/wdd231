Four Against Darkness Web Companion

Site Name

The site is called Four Against Darkness Web Companion. It identifies the project as a digital tool that supports and enhances gameplay for the solo RPG Four Against Darkness.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to provide Four Against Darkness players with a streamlined, digital companion that simplifies gameplay tasks—mapping, dice rolling, and party tracking—so they can spend less time managing mechanics and more time immersed in the adventure.

Scenarios

  • How can I quickly roll dice with special rules (like exploding sixes) without manual math?
  • Where can I track my party’s stats, equipment, and dungeon progress in one place?
  • How do I record notes about encounters and treasure so I don’t lose them between sessions?

Color Scheme

The site uses a fantasy dungeon aesthetic:

Dark Gray (#2C2C2C) – background, dungeon feel
Parchment Beige (#F5DEB3) – notes editor, headers
Deep Crimson (#8B0000) – dice results, highlights

Typography
